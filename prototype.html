JOIN 多表连接

2/5

INNER JOIN 内部连接

返回两个表中满足连接条件的匹配行。

SELECT o.order_id, c.customer_name
FROM orders o
INNER JOIN customers c
  ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id;

💡 只返回有关联的数据行

LEFT JOIN 左连接

返回左表所有行，右表无匹配时填充 NULL。

SELECT c.customer_id, o.order_id
FROM customers c
LEFT JOIN orders o
  ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id;

💡 适合查「哪些客户从未下单」
